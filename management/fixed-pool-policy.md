# Explicit fixed-pool site exception

On 2026-10-22 the owner explicitly authorized LAC-01, which has no spare public IPs, to distribute RS accounts evenly by account count over its existing IPv4 and IPv6 exits. Assign new accounts to the less populated exit and then keep each assignment fixed. This describes account counts, not a guarantee of equal traffic or bandwidth.

Each account must retain distinct fixed proxy authentication, durable registration and an authenticated real-exit check before login. Native OAuth start, plugin authorization-code login, native OAuth completion and refresh-token save/readback remain required. Preserve full-directory deduplication, account/workspace identity, exact three-field notes, manual pause and in-flight protections. Uncertain outcomes retain reservations and require reconciliation; never replay login, silently allocate another proxy, use another account's authentication or fall back to direct networking.

This exception applies only to LAC-01 and does not relax other sites' independent IPv6 ownership or permanent retirement rules. Existing account bindings must not be rebalanced or services restarted simply to improve counts. Private deployment addresses, authentication, account records and receipts do not belong in this repository.
